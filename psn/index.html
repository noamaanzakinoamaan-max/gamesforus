PSN account questions

Are HeavenDigital PSN listings redeem codes?

No. Listings clearly labelled as accounts provide access to an account on which the listed game is already available. Read the tier and region shown on the product before paying.

What is the difference between PSN Secondary and Primary?

Secondary is the lower-cost tier and is generally played from the supplied profile. Primary enables console sharing so other profiles on that console can play. Exact instructions and limitations are confirmed before payment.

Do PSN accounts have a warranty?

HeavenDigital states a 30-day replacement-or-refund warranty for eligible account problems not caused by the buyer. Review the current policy and keep your order ID.
