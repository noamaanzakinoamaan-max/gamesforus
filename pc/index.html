PC account questions

What is a PC Offline account tier?

It is generally intended for single-player use on the supplied account with offline-mode requirements. Confirm the exact launcher and setup rules on the listing.

What is the PC Online tier?

An Online tier is offered on some products for features that require connectivity. Availability, supported modes and price vary by game, so verify before paying.

Will I receive a Steam key?

Not for a listing labelled as a game account. Account listings provide supplied-account access; they are not redeemable keys. The product page identifies what is being sold.
