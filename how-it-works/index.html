From listing to setup

Read the full listing

Confirm product type, title, platform, tier, region, term, stock and final price. An account listing is not a redeem code.

Place the order

Add the product to cart. Checkout creates an order ID and a UPI payment link or UPI ID.

Confirm on WhatsApp

Send the order ID after paying. Staff verify the payment in the receiving account; screenshots alone are not treated as proof.

Receive credentials

For account products, supplied credentials and product-specific instructions arrive on WhatsApp.

Follow setup guidance

Follow the current console or launcher steps. Ask support before changing security or sharing details.

Keep the order ID

The ID is needed for tracking, support and eligible warranty requests.

Buyer safety checklist

  • Verify that the domain is heavendigital.store before ordering.
  • Never share passwords or one-time passwords for your personal PSN, Steam, Xbox, email or bank accounts.
  • Confirm the exact access duration; do not interpret “long-term” as guaranteed lifetime access.
  • Understand whether platform account transfer or sharing conflicts with the platform's terms.
  • Save the order ID, payment reference and written product description.
Independent reseller disclosure: HeavenDigital is not affiliated with Sony, Microsoft, Valve or game publishers. Account resale or sharing may violate platform rules and can carry suspension or access-loss risk even when a seller offers its own warranty.

Ordering questions

How quickly are orders delivered?

HeavenDigital states that most verified orders are delivered in about 10 minutes during its published support hours, but actual time can vary.

What should I check before paying?

Confirm the product type, platform, region, account tier, subscription duration, current price, stock, platform-term risk and warranty conditions.

What information should I keep?

Keep the generated HeavenDigital order ID and your payment confirmation. Never send passwords or one-time passwords for your personal accounts.
