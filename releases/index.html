Release questions

Are release dates on HeavenDigital final?

Publishers move dates, sometimes at short notice. The calendar tracks announced dates and flags them as subject to change. When a date moves, tracking pages and alert subscribers are updated.

Can I preorder a game before release day?

Preorder-style listings are clearly labelled and availability depends on sourcing. The listing states whether delivery is at launch or later, and the price applies when the order is fulfilled.

How do I get told when a tracked game launches?

Add the game to your wishlist, or enable release alerts on the account alerts tab. Notifications appear in the bell centre and, with push enabled, on your device.
